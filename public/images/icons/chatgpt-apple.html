  • Amazing for college
    03/28/2025
    MangoDaBango

    I had taken a break from school for a few years and I was nervous to get back and finish my degree because the few classes I had left were graduate level classes and an upper level statistics class (never taken it before in my life, plus I couldn’t afford to take the beginning stats class). I decided to just start off by taking one class to start (the hardest one) which was statistics. If I didn’t have this app I would have failed a long time ago. I have an A+ in the class and the semester ends in one month. I asked this app to quite literally take every concept that was thrown at us and “dumb it down for me” and it made learning all those concepts so much easier! I can even put in the text from the class PowerPoint and upload my homework relating to that PowerPoint (because if you just ask it mathematical statistics questions some times it answers them to advanced then where we were at in that point in time) and it would break the problem down step by step for me with explanations. It would give me practice problems that look like my homework so I could prepare for the tests. Not only does it help with school, but I have asked it a million other random questions that I normally would have typed into safari and then had to read a bunch of different articles to get my answer (and even then I could still have read incorrect information). But this just spits out amazing answers to questions with very helpful explanations when needed. 10/10

  • Less value now
    11/06/2025
    Nai Rot

    My experience with this app has changed significantly over time. In the past, when I used it, I could usually receive straightforward answers to my questions very quickly, which made the overall experience very efficient and satisfying. However, recently I've noticed a considerable shift in how the app responds. Now, instead of providing an immediate answer, the app often seems to initiate a sort of interactive game or activity when I'm typing my questions. It doesn't give a direct response right away; instead, it starts engaging me in some kind of playful interaction or puzzle without clearly addressing the query I posed. This new behavior can be quite confusing and frustrating because I often just want a simple, clear answer, especially when I am in a hurry or need quick information. Moreover, this change seems to be part of a broader set of issues that have arisen in the app lately. I've experienced other problems such as slower response times, occasional crashes, or features that used to work smoothly now functioning improperly. These issues have collectively impacted my overall user experience, making it less reliable and less user-friendly than it was before. It's disappointing because the app initially performed very well and met my expectations, but recent updates or changes appear to have introduced these new challenges. I hope the developers will address these problems soon, as I enjoyed the app's previous performance and would like to return to that level of efficiency and simplicity.
